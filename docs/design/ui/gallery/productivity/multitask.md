## Drag

Let users intuitively share content in a large, unconfined workspace that makes dragging natural and comfortable---between widespread views in the same app or, in multi-window mode, between apps.

![](https://developer.android.com/static/images/large-screens/gallery/samples/productivity/gallery_productivity_dragdrop.webp)

## Multi-window

Get apps working together side by side in multi-window mode, with space for note-taking while reading an article or watching a video across any device size, such as phones, foldables, and tablets.

![](https://developer.android.com/static/images/large-screens/gallery/samples/productivity/gallery_productivity_multiwindow.webp)

## Implement a supporting pane

> Documentation index: https://developer.android.com/games/llms.txt

Differentiated games use the large screen and foldable form factors to
their full potential. Differentiated games offer a premium user experience
that's productive and delightful.

![Depiction of the three quality tiers as layers stacked vertically with the top tier highlighted.](https://developer.android.com/static/images/docs/quality-guidelines/tier-1/tier_1_header.png)

Because Tier 1 games are highly differentiated, the Tier 1 guidelines are
organized by game *experiences*---the type of game you're creating or the
type of device you're targeting.

Choose the experiences that are right for your game:
- [![](https://developer.android.com/static/images/docs/quality-guidelines/tier-1/experiences/dual-screen-devices.svg)
  Foldables](https://developer.android.com/games/large-screen/foldables)

With [Google Play Instant](https://developer.android.com/topic/google-play-instant), people can use an app or game without
installing it first. Increase engagement with your Android app or gain more
installs by exposing your instant app across Google Play Store and the
Google Play Games app.

![google play instant
icon](https://developer.android.com/static/images/games/engines/cocos/google-play-instant-icon.png)

## How to publish your game as Google Play Instant app

In Cocos Creator, just check the **Google Play Instant** option in the Android
Build panel. You can then publish your game with the Google Play Instant
ability.

![build panel googleplay instant
app](https://developer.android.com/static/images/games/engines/cocos/build-panel-google-instant.png)

## Things to be noted

Note the following points when publishing your game as a Google Play Instant
app.

1. Make sure you have Android Studio v4.0 or above installed.

2. Google Play Instant can only be launched on devices with Android 6.0 or
   higher with Google Service Framework installed.

3. Before compiling in Android Studio, developers should also have downloaded
   and installed Google Play Instant Development SDK (for Windows) or Instant
   Apps Development SDK (for Mac). If downloads were unsuccessful, you may have
   to set up HTTP proxies for Android Studio. ![googlelay instant sdk
   download](https://developer.android.com/static/images/games/engines/cocos/google-instant-sdk-dl.png)

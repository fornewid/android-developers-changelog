<br />

Android XR apps can include both immersive and augmented experiences on a
variety of different XR devices.

Review the quality guidelines that apply to the type of experiences you're
building to verify that your app provides a great experience for your users
regardless of the device that they use.

## Immersive experiences

Applicable XR devices Immersive experiences can run on these types of XR devices. [Learn about XR device types →](https://developer.android.com/develop/xr/devices) ![](https://developer.android.com/static/images/develop/xr/xr-headsets-icon.svg) XR Headsets [](https://developer.android.com/develop/xr/devices#xr-headsets) ![](https://developer.android.com/static/images/develop/xr/xr-glasses-icon.svg) Wired XR Glasses [](https://developer.android.com/develop/xr/devices#xr-glasses) [Learn about XR device types →](https://developer.android.com/develop/xr/devices)

Review the
[XR app quality guidelines for immersive experiences](https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/android-xr/immersive) if
you're building any of these types of app experiences:

- Fully-immersive experiences for fully-virtual space.
- Semi-immersive experiences that blend digital content with the user's environment.

## Augmented experiences

Applicable XR devices Augmented experiences can run on these types of XR devices. [Learn about XR device types →](https://developer.android.com/develop/xr/devices) ![](https://developer.android.com/static/images/develop/xr/ai-glasses-icon.svg) Audio \&  
Display Glasses [](https://developer.android.com/develop/xr/devices#audio-display) [Learn about XR device types →](https://developer.android.com/develop/xr/devices)

Review the
[XR app quality guidelines for augmented experiences](https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/android-xr/augmented) if
you're building lightweight, augmented experiences that help users on the go, at
home, or at work.

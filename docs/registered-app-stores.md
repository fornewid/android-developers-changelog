### Registered App Stores

A Registered App Store is a third-party app store that registers with Google to access additional platform capabilities, including one-tap installs, while upholding core ecosystem trust, security, and performance standards. [![](http://developer.android.com/static/images/picto-icons/learn.svg)](http://developer.android.com/registered-app-stores/overview)

> [!NOTE]
> **Note:** Gemini in Android Studio is only available in the latest stable channel version of Android Studio and major versions released in the previous 10 months. If you are using an older version of Android Studio, you will need to update to use Gemini. For more information, see [Android Studio and Cloud services
> compatibility](https://developer.android.com/studio/releases#service-compat).

Gemini in Android Studio is your AI-powered coding companion for Android
development. It helps you be more productive by answering your development
queries, generating code, finding relevant resources, and encouraging best
practices. Most importantly, Gemini in Android Studio is uniquely equipped to
accelerate Android development---it can help you mock up and troubleshoot Compose
UIs, fix Gradle build errors, analyze crashes through integrations with Logcat
and App Quality Insights, and more.
[Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zGK1vIX87vw)

Gemini in Android Studio is available for individual developers and businesses:

- [Individual tier](https://developer.android.com/studio/gemini/feature-comparison#individuals)
  - Free
  - [API key](https://developer.android.com/studio/gemini/add-api-key)
  - [Google One membership](https://one.google.com/intl/en_us/about/google-ai-plans/)
- [Business tier](https://developer.android.com/studio/gemini/feature-comparison#businesses)

  Available through a subscription to [Gemini Enterprise](https://cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise) or membership in
  the [Google Developer Program](https://developers.google.com/program).

> [!NOTE]
> **Note:** If you're interested in agent-driven development for Android using agents outside of Android Studio, use [Android CLI](https://developer.android.com/tools/agents/android-cli).

## What is the right tier for me?

Choose the product tier and edition that are right for you.

### Individuals

If you're an individual freelance developer, student, or hobbyist, we recommend
the Gemini in Android Studio [free edition](https://developer.android.com/studio/gemini/feature-comparison#individuals). At no cost, you get access to a
lightweight version of Gemini with a small context window, which is appropriate
for most tasks. If you need to execute more complex tasks that would benefit
from Gemini Pro's full 1M token context window, you can
[add a Gemini API key](https://developer.android.com/studio/gemini/add-api-key) and pay per token.

Or you can [subscribe to Google One](https://one.google.com/about/) and get 2 TB or more of cloud
storage, higher rate limits, and additional benefits such as Gemini in Google
Docs and Gmail, access to Veo, and higher usage limits on products like Nano
Banana and NotebookLM. [Find the right Google One plan for you](https://one.google.com/intl/en_us/about/google-ai-plans/#compare-plans).

> [!NOTE]
> **Note:** If you already subscribe to Google One, you automatically get the Gemini in Android Studio added capabilities and benefits at no additional cost.

### Businesses

If you're a professional developer who works as part of a team, ask your admin
to consider subscribing to the [business tier](https://developer.android.com/studio/gemini/feature-comparison#businesses). The business tier includes
additional features that make it easier to collaborate with your teammates while
incorporating your company's best practices and standards, including but not
limited to:

- Higher quota
- Centralized administrative controls
- Data residency and security, including VPC Service Controls

For more details about the different offerings, see [Feature comparison](https://developer.android.com/studio/gemini/feature-comparison).

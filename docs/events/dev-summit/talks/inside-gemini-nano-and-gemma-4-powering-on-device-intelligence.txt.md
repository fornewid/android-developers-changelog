# Inside Gemini Nano and Gemma 4 powering on-device intelligence - Speakers: Sara Smoot (Engineering Lead Gemma) - Track: Intelligent Apps - Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ekS4_gV6Ojc - Page: https://developer.android.com/events/dev-summit/talks/inside-gemini-nano-and-gemma-4-powering-on-device-intelligence ## About the talk Gemma 4 brings capable, multimodal AI directly to Android devices. In this session, we'll trace how Gemma evolved into a model family built for efficiency and explore what the edge-optimized E2B and E4B models can do on-device, from vision and audio understanding to agentic function calling. We'll cover the architecture behind that efficiency, including Per-Layer Embeddings (PLE), and show how Gemma and Gemini Nano work together through AICore. You'll learn how Quantization-Aware Training (QAT) preserves model capability within 1.1--3.5 GB RAM footprints, while Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) accelerates decoding speeds 1.5x--3x for responsive, real-time experiences. ## Summary - Gemma 4 models expand on-device multimodal capabilities with 128K-token context on E2B/E4B under an Apache 2 license. - Multi-token prediction drafter models cut decode latency by 40% and save 30% to 40% energy. They share the base model's KV cache to avoid regenerating prefills. - Quantization-aware training compresses the custom mobile quantization checkpoint to under 1 GB using targeted 2-bit quantization. - Android applications can integrate the models hands-off through AICore as Gemini Nano 4, or bundle them with LiteRT for custom fine-tunes. ## Resources - \[Gemma 4 model

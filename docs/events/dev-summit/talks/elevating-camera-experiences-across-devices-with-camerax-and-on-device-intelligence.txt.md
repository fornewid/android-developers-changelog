# Elevating camera experiences across devices with CameraX and on-device intelligence - Speakers: Aman Choudhary (Developer Relations Engineer), Scott Nien (Software Engineer) - Track: Apps foundation - Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr-9hwgo10Q - Page: https://developer.android.com/events/dev-summit/talks/elevating-camera-experiences-across-devices-with-camerax-and-on-device-intelligence ## About the talk Users expect third-party apps to capture photos and videos with the same high fidelity as their device's native camera, regardless of lighting conditions or hardware tier. Delivering this consistency across the expanding Android ecosystem comes with key architectural considerations. This session demonstrates how to construct robust, professional capture pipelines that maximize device capabilities while remaining reliable across all form factors. We will explore the latest advancements in CameraX, showing you how to integrate cutting-edge capabilities like Motion Photos and on-device AI enhancements through Google Play services, alongside spatial experiences using the Jetpack XR SDK. We will also look at our CameraX skill, which allows the coding agent of your choice to automatically implement the concepts in this talk, and more! ## Summary - Jetpack CameraX is the recommended camera library for Android apps as Camera1 is deprecated. Play policy enforcements will begin disabling Camera1 next year in Android 18. - \`CameraXViewfinder\` adapts camera previews across different form factors such as foldables. In CameraX 1.7, it adds gesture controls and Screen Flash support. - Audio \& Display Glasses run apps on a host phone while exposing cameras on both devices. Passing \`ProjectedDeviceContext\` allows CameraX to capture images directly from the glasses. - The Feature Group API helps apps select supported combinations of camera capabilities. Starting with CameraX 1.6, it supports combining 4K recording with Video Stabilization. - On-device machine learning analyzes camera previews and captured media through ML Kit and MediaPipe. Developers can run tasks ranging from real-time gesture detection to generative image descriptions. ## Resources - \[Camera talk resources and migration guide\](https://goo.gle/ads-26-camera-talk-res)

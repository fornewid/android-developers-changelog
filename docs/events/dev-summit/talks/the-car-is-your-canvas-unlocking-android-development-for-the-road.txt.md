# The car is your canvas: Unlocking Android development for the road - Speakers: Paul Lammertsma (Developer Relations Engineer), Thomas Weathers (Developer Relations Engineer) - Track: Apps foundation - Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8YkU3aPbQYA - Page: https://developer.android.com/events/dev-summit/talks/the-car-is-your-canvas-unlocking-android-development-for-the-road ## About the talk As in-car displays expand across Android Auto and Android Automotive OS vehicles, developers face a unique dual challenge: delivering rich, adaptive experiences while parked, and driver-safe, template-driven experiences while driving. In this comprehensive 30-minute technical session, we explore the end-to-end architecture and development approaches to adapting Android apps to cars. From the fundamentals of designing apps for use in cars, to the newest approaches for building media experiences that shift from audio to video as the car shifts from drive to park, you'll leave with everything you need to know to get your app on the road. ## Summary - Android for Cars targets two platforms: Android Auto and Android Automotive OS. One projects from a phone, while the other runs directly on the vehicle. - The Car App Library provides templates for driving-optimized interfaces that adapt to various car hardware configurations. - Parked car apps rely on standard Android activities for rich experiences like video streaming, games, and web browsers. - Car App Library 1.9 brings new UI components and brand customization tokens. It also expands media template support fully to Android Automotive OS. - Park-to-drive capabilities dynamically launch native video activities when parked, then switch back to media templates to maintain uninterrupted audio when driving. ## Resources - \[Android CLI\](https://goo.gle/android-cli) - \[Android for Cars documentation\](https://goo.gle/android-cars) - \[Build games for cars

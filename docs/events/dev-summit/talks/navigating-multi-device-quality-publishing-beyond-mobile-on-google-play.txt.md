# Navigating multi-device quality \& publishing beyond mobile on Google Play - Speakers: Jan Kleinert (Developer Relations Engineer), Faustine Liao (DFX Operations Lead) - Track: Tools \& Performance - Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=93NhXMuUDJo - Page: https://developer.android.com/events/dev-summit/talks/navigating-multi-device-quality-publishing-beyond-mobile-on-google-play ## About the talk As the Android ecosystem expands across wrists, living rooms, vehicles, and XR, the opportunity to reach users on new screens is growing. However, distributing across Wear OS, Android TV, Android Auto, Android Automotive OS, or Android XR introduces distinct considerations for testing, store listings, and app reviews. This session explores how to effectively utilize Google Play Console for non-mobile form factors to help streamline a predictable multi-device launch. We will cover key distribution decisions, including how to structure track strategies and package names, configure targeted testing tracks, and tailor store listings. You'll get a practical walkthrough of form-factor App Quality guidelines to help avoid common review pitfalls and publish on Google Play with greater confidence. ## Summary - Modular app bundles isolate form-factor releases from your mobile app. If a form-factor review fails, only that specific bundle is blocked while your mobile release publishes as intended. - Play Console tracks offer flexible publishing paths, with exceptions for Wear OS and Android Auto. Wear OS requires a dedicated track, while Android Auto must use the standard mobile track. - Internal testing tracks distribute builds to up to 100 testers without triggering a Play review. Closed and open testing tracks do trigger reviews, helping you resolve hurdles before launch. - Form-factor review rejections often stem from missed device requirements, such as car app category limits or absent screenshots. Provide valid demo account credentials in Play Console whenever your app requires login. ## Resources - \[Android user experience guidelines\](https://developer.android.com/quality/user-experience) -

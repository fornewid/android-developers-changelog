# Extend your app to Gemini and Search with the Connected Apps Platform - Speakers: Kateryna Semenova (Staff Developer Relations Engineer), Mai Lowe (Group Product Manager) - Track: Intelligent Apps - Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-4G-4w9FKDw - Page: https://developer.android.com/events/dev-summit/talks/extend-your-app-to-gemini-and-search-with-the-connected-apps-platform ## About the talk The new Connected Apps Platform lets you integrate your app's services directly into Gemini, AI Mode in Search, and the Android intelligence system. This session provides a practical guide to getting started. Using code examples and learning from real-life case studies, we'll walk through the development lifecycle: building, testing, and publishing your first connected app to reach users within their AI-powered workflows. By the end of this session, you'll be able to: - Integrate your service with the Connected Apps Platform. - Test your integration across different AI surfaces. - Publish your app's features into users' AI workflows. ## Summary - The Connected Apps Platform provides a single entry point to launch app capabilities across Gemini and Search using declarative schemas. It will open in phases, starting with a preview for US developers. - Integrations use the Model Context Protocol (MCP) standard through either remote servers or local on-device servers via the AppFunctions Jetpack library. Remote servers can also return custom UI rendered inside an iframe. - Confirmations scale with risk to protect user trust. Developers mark tools as read-only or with \`destructiveHint\`, helping Gemini ask for explicit confirmation when actions modify or delete data. - Gemini Developer Mode lets you inspect tool calls and latency against public endpoints. It is available now to developers in the US. - Google Connected Console handles the production rollout for approved developers. You configure endpoints, manage testers across selected surfaces, and submit your app for review. ## Resources - \[Connected Apps preview sign-up\](https://developers.google.com/connected-apps) - \[AppFunctions integration guide\](https://developer.android.com/ai/appfunctions) ## Topics gemini, search, connected-apps, mcp, appfunctions, android ## Transcript ### Introduction

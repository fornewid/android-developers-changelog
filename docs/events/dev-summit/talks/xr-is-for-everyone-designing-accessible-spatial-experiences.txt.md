# XR is for everyone: Designing accessible spatial experiences - Speakers: Jazmin Cano (User Experience Research Lead) - Track: Android XR - Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCuqj4bwvB4 - Page: https://developer.android.com/events/dev-summit/talks/xr-is-for-everyone-designing-accessible-spatial-experiences ## About the talk When we say XR is for everyone, that principle has to guide how we build from day one. In this introductory guide for developers new to XR, Jazmin Cano shares the foundational accessibility principles honed at Owlchemy Labs. Engine-agnostic and universally applicable to both games and apps, this session explores how early consideration of motor, visual, auditory, and cognitive needs leads to better experiences for all users. Discover how to build an inclusive development culture, conduct meaningful testing, and design spatial interactions that remove barriers without sacrificing quality. ## Summary - Spatial interfaces risk excluding users if access needs are omitted during early design. Consider accessibility across physical and cognitive categories from the very start. - Disability affects 1.3 billion people worldwide, including roughly 20% of gamers. Designing for specific access needs often benefits a wider audience in temporary or situational contexts. - Physical accessibility features reduce body strain and accommodate limited mobility. Useful options include scalable environments (such as small human mode) and artificial snap turning. - Vision and auditory aids provide critical cues across alternate senses. Useful implementations include high-contrast object glows and directional subtitles. - Testing with diverse groups helps teams iterate effectively on spatial designs. Involve people across varied disabilities and age groups throughout development. - Developers should prototype inclusively from the beginning, test with diverse users, and make an immediate commitment to implement at least one accessible feature. ## Resources - \[XR Accessibility Project\](https://xraccessibility.github.io/) - \[GAConf\](https://www.gaconf.com/) - \[GAConf

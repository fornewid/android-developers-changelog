# Decoding Play Policy AMA - Speakers: Paul Hua (Trust \& Safety Google Play), Bohdan Kosenko (Trust \& Safety Google Play), Jan Kleinert (Developer Relations Engineer) - Track: Tools \& Performance - Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OaZ-_hTaZIA - Page: https://developer.android.com/events/dev-summit/talks/decoding-play-policy-ama ## About the talk An open and honest panel about Play policies to keep your app compliant and quickly published. ## Summary - Reviewers need working credentials to test gated areas such as subscriptions and paywalls. Add credentials in Play Console under Sign-in details, and ensure they bypass two-factor authentication or captchas. - App declarations in Play Console must match the manifests of active bundles across all tracks. Deactivate obsolete version codes so abandoned testing builds do not trigger policy rejections. - App developers are responsible for content produced by third-party generative AI and user data handled by SDKs. Use mechanisms to filter AI output and check the Google Play SDK Index. - Appeals let you communicate with real reviewers after rejections or request extra time to fix policy warnings. Do not resubmit an unchanged app, as repetitive rejections can trigger removals or suspensions. - The advance notice form in Play Console allows developers to submit proof of permission before publishing. Providing authorization documentation upfront protects your submission against impersonation policy rejections. ## Resources - \[Developer Policy Center\](https://goo.gle/play-policy-center) - \[Policy Bytes playlist\](https://goo.gle/policy-bytes-playlist) - \[Play policy insights in Android Studio\](https://d.android.com/studio/publish/insights) - \[Android

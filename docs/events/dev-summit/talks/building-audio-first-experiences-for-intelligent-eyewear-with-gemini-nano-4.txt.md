# Building audio-first experiences for intelligent eyewear with Gemini Nano 4 - Speakers: Amy Zeppenfeld (Developer Relations Engineer \| Android XR), Alice Zheng (Senior Product Manager) - Track: Android XR - Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tW3mg0iyi_w - Page: https://developer.android.com/events/dev-summit/talks/building-audio-first-experiences-for-intelligent-eyewear-with-gemini-nano-4 ## About the talk Discover how to build robust, hands-free audio experiences powered by on-device AI. We'll cover everything you need: setting up your glasses activity, capturing mic and camera feeds, and using voice along with other audio cues as your ultimate UI. Through an exciting real-world showcase of the open-source XR Language Teacher built in the Google AI Edge Gallery app, learn to orchestrate Gemini Nano 4 (built on Gemma 4 and optimized for Android devices), and ML Kit, and leverage the LiteRT runtime to build fast XR experiences that hear, see, and speak naturally. ## Summary - The Jetpack Projected library (alpha) bridges Android phones to intelligent eyewear. Adding a projected activity to your existing app projects content to display or audio-only glasses. - ML Kit introduces alpha on-device speech libraries for audio-first interactions on select devices. It offers Text to Speech with 8 voices across 21 locales and Speech Recognition with a Gemini Nano advanced mode on Pixel 10. - Gemini Nano 4 processes camera frames and transcribed questions together in one pass. It powers offline experiences by chaining on-device speech models locally on supported flagship phones. - A two-pass design prioritizes real-time audio latency on smart glasses. Pass 1 streams spoken text using inline XML tags for mid-sentence locale switching, while Pass 2 extracts structured data in the background. - The open-source XR Language Teacher implementation ships in the Google AI Edge Gallery app. Developers can adapt its code for other multimodal assistant use cases. ## Resources - \[Android XR Developer Documentation\](https://goo.gle/xr-dev) - \[Android XR

# Expanding Android developer verification in 2027

2 min read ![](https://developer.android.com/static/blog/assets/ABL_139_Android_developer_verification_Going_global_together_Strapi_01_png_12aa1e5aae_ZkNaef.webp) 19 Nov 2026 [![View Matthew Forsythe's profile](https://developer.android.com/static/blog/assets/matthew_9c798f0c1d_1FlQLe.webp)](https://developer.android.com/blog/authors/matthew-forsythe.md.txt) [Matthew Forsythe](https://developer.android.com/blog/authors/matthew-forsythe.md.txt) Director, Product Management, Android App Safety We [announced](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/08/elevating-android-security.html) Android developer verification in 2025 as an extra layer of security that helps deter bad actors and makes it harder for them to repeatedly spread harm. After over a year of joint preparation with industry leaders, regional partners, app distributors, and Android communities, our user protections successfully went live on September 30 for participating stores across Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand. Today, we're sharing our plans for expanding globally, alongside tools we launched this summer based on your feedback.

Our **global rollout will begin in January 2027** in partnershipwith seven app distributors **(Google Play** , **Galaxy Store, Honor, Oppo, Xiaomi, Transsion, and Vivo).** User protections will continue to gradually expand to all countries to cover **all installation sources** on [certified Android devices](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/guides/faq.md.txt#versions) over several months.

If you have not verified your identity and registered your apps yet, you can complete the process today. It takes **less than ten minutes to register an app**, and ensures your users will continue to have a seamless installation experience everywhere these protections are live.

### Progress across the Android ecosystem

Thank you to all the developers who have completed verification and registered their apps. That collective effort is showing strong momentum across the platform: **Millions of apps have been registered** **globally**since verification opened for everyone in March. These apps represent over 99% of all Android app installs globally, which users can download via any distribution channel they choose.

### Features built with your feedback

Throughout this rollout, your feedback has directly shaped the tools we built to make meeting these requirements straightforward for different developer needs:

- **Expanded device limits for limited distribution accounts** : In August, we launched [limited distribution accounts](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/guides/limited-distribution.md.txt) for student and hobbyists who want to share apps without registration fees or verifying a government ID. To offer more testing flexibility tailored to your scope, we expanded the sharing limit **from 20 to 50 authorized devices**.
- **APIs to automate registration workflows** : For teams managing multiple apps, we launched the [Android Developer ID Status API](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/guides/check-registration-status.md.txt) and [Android Developer Console API](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/guides/developer-console-api.md.txt) to speed up app registration directly within your continuous integration pipelines.
- **Advanced flow for power users** : We built an [advanced flow](https://support.google.com/android/answer/17588095?sjid=1132776567470189201-SA) that allows experienced users to install unregistered apps when they choose to accept the risk, while keeping critical guardrails in place against social engineering scams for everyday users.

### How to get ready

Getting your apps ready takes just a few steps based on how you prefer to distribute. Android remains committed to enabling you to distribute your apps through whichever channels you choose:

- Developers who only distribute outside Google Play: Create your [Android Developer Console](https://android.google.com/developerconsole) account to verify your identity and register your apps.
  - Students and hobbyists: Use limited distribution accounts for lightweight testing and sharing apps up to 50 authorized devices.
  - Developers who publish apps on open-source registries: Review our **new** [**guide**](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/guides/open-source-app-registration.md.txt) to learn how to register both existing and new apps.
  - Android app distributors: Review our **new** [**guide**](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/guides/distributor-resources.md.txt) for resources to help your developer communities register apps.
- Google Play developers: Check your package registration status on [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console) and register any unregistered apps, including those you distribute outside of Play, so your users continue to have an uninterrupted installation experience.

Thank you for your ongoing feedback and partnership in bringing higher security standards to all Android users. For step-by-step walkthroughs and answers to common questions, visit our [website](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification).

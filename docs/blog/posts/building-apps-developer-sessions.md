# Developing for Today: 50+ New Developer Sessions on Building Apps Today

1 min read ![](https://developer.android.com/static/blog/assets/ABL_155_Strapi_1_f48145e2db_Z2jyq4w.webp) 02 Nov 2026 [![View Florina Muntenescu's profile](https://developer.android.com/static/blog/assets/unnamed_27_1d76df3c3b_2gwEzo.webp)](https://developer.android.com/blog/authors/florina-muntenescu) [Florina Muntenescu](https://developer.android.com/blog/authors/florina-muntenescu) Dev Rel Manager, Developer Relations After a four-year hiatus, Android Dev Summit is officially BACK, with events in both Mountain View and London last week. Over 900 attendees joined our event spaces for a keynote about our outlook on the future, demo sessions, dozens of workshops, a hackathon, and for community discussions focused on ideas and feedback on Android platform.

But don't worry: We are bringing tools, APIs and resources from the event to everyone, no matter where you are. Today, we are releasing over 50 sessions from Googler and partner experts at ADS, crossing App Foundations, Intelligent Apps, Tools and Performance, and Android XR.

These sessions are available on YouTube and also on [d.android.com/events/dev-summit](http://d.android.com/events/dev-summit), which includes full, downloadable transcripts for every talk. The transcripts are enhanced with the content from the session slides so you, and your agents, are able to get the full context of the talk. You can download it so you can easily drop the transcript in your agent of choice and already integrate the takeaways in your app.

To make it easier to search and interact with the sessions, we've created Gemini Notebooks for each track, containing the enhanced transcript.

Find four tracks of sessions and their transcripts below:

- Apps Foundations: [YouTube playlist](https://goo.gle/ADS26-Apps-Foundation), [Gemini Notebook](https://notebook.google.com/notebook/12edfaaa-e2fd-4075-b7f4-633374b0937c)
- Intelligent Apps: [YouTube playlist](https://goo.gle/ADS26-Intelligent-Apps), [Gemini Notebook](https://notebook.google.com/notebook/e78e0a17-9c6e-4504-8add-4ca3a69f2ac3)
- Tools and Performance: [YouTube playlist](https://goo.gle/ADS26-Tools-and-Perf), [Gemini Notebook](https://notebook.google.com/notebook/a3cafe10-41cf-4969-bf15-4774acecfcda)
- Android XR: [YouTube playlist](https://goo.gle/ADS26-XR), [Gemini Notebook](https://notebook.google.com/notebook/189939ee-4eb6-45ba-a32e-fd9f21f0fb40)

Please let us know any feedback you have on what we are building and any other resources we can provide. Happy watching!

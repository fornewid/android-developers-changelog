> Documentation index: https://developer.android.com/distribute/llms.txt

Review the age-related protections and tools that Play makes available in each
region where you distribute your app. You're responsible for making sure your
app complies with all applicable regional regulations and Google Play policies.

## Protections by region

The following table lists the age-related protections and tools Play makes
available in certain regions. To find a specific country or region, use the
filter field.

| Country or region | Protections on Play |
|---|---|
| Global | The [Play Age Signals API](https://developer.android.com/google/play/age-signals/overview.md.txt) provides apps with access to age range information from users if a user [chooses to share their age range](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/17232873). |
| Australia, EEA, Singapore, Switzerland, and the UK | Play restricts users identified as minors from downloading apps rated as 18+ under its [local age rating](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859655#How_content_ratings_are_used). |
| Brazil | - \[Starting January 2027\] The Play Age Signals API returns age information for users, on a mandatory basis, in Brazil to help developers meet requirements under the Digital ECA. For more information, see [Distributing your app in Brazil](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6223646#digital_eca_requirements). - Play restricts users identified as minors from downloading apps rated as 18+ under its [local age rating](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859655#How_content_ratings_are_used). |
| United States (Alabama) | Details coming soon. |
| United States (California) | \[Starting January 2027\] The [Play Age Signals API](https://developer.android.com/google/play/age-signals/overview.md.txt) returns age range information for users on a mandatory basis. For more information, see [Changes to Google Play for upcoming app store bills for users in applicable US states](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16569691). |
| United States (Texas) | Google Play verifies the ages of eligible users and the [Play Age Signals API](https://developer.android.com/google/play/age-signals/overview.md.txt) returns age range and significant change approval status signals for eligible users. For more information, see [Changes to Google Play for upcoming app store bills for users in applicable US states](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16569691). |

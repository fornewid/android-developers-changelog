# Regional age-related protections on Play Stay organized with collections Save and categorize content based on your preferences.





Review the age-related protections and tools that Play makes available in each
region where you distribute your app. You're responsible for making sure your
app complies with all applicable regional regulations and Google Play policies.

## Protections by region

The following table lists the age-related protections and tools Play makes
available in certain regions. To find a specific country or region, use the
filter field.

| Country or region | Protections on Play |
| --- | --- |
| Global | The Play Age Signals API (coming in Q4 2026) provides apps with access to age range information from users with age range sharing enabled. |
| Australia, Brazil, EEA, Singapore, Switzerland, and the UK | Play restricts users identified as minors from downloading apps rated as 18+ under its local age rating. |
| United States | * In US states with regulations that require app stores to verify users' ages, Play verifies the ages of users in accordance with these regulations. * In US states that require app stores to share users' age ranges, the Play Age Signals API will return age information for users subject to this requirement. |

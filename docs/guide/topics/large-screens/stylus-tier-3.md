TIER 3 --- Adaptive ready

> [!NOTE]
> **Objective:** Make your app [adaptive ready](https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/adaptive-app-quality/tier-3) by meeting the [Stylus_Basic](https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/adaptive-app-quality/tier-3#Stylus_Basic) and [Stylus_Text_Input](https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/adaptive-app-quality/tier-3#Stylus_Text_Input) requirements of the [Adaptive app quality guidelines](https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/adaptive-app-quality).

Productivity and creativity apps on large screen devices are at their best when
the apps support stylus input.

Enable your users to take advantage of the natural, intuitive interactivity a
stylus provides for:

- Writing and editing text
- Scrolling through lists, pickers, and other scrollable content

TIER 1 --- Adaptive differentiated

> [!NOTE]
> **Objective:** Make your app [adaptive differentiated](https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/adaptive-app-quality/tier-1) by meeting the [Drag_Drop_Support](https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/adaptive-app-quality/experiences/desktop#Drag_Drop_Support) requirement of the [Adaptive app quality guidelines](https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/adaptive-app-quality).

Differentiate your app on large screens by supporting drag and drop within the
app and, in multi‑window mode, between apps.

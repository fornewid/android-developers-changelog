+## Edit Game Stats
+
+If you already have existing Game Stats and want to edit:
+
+1. In the [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console), select a game.
+2. Go to **Play Games Services - Game Stats** page (**Grow users \> Play Games Services \> Setup and management \> Game Stats**).
+3. To edit an **Event** :
+   1. In the **Events configuration** section, select an event.
+   2. You can add or delete **Event properties** .
+      - You cannot edit the **Event Name**.
+   3. Click **Save changes**.
+4. To edit a **Stat** :
+   1. In the **Game Stats configuration** section, select a stat.
+   2. You can edit the **Stat name** , **Stat description** , and **Icon file
+      name** fields.
+      - You cannot edit the **Stat ID** , **Progression direction** . **Stat unit** , and **Associated event details**.
+   3. Click **Save changes**.
+5. In the **Play Games Services - Game Stats** page, the edited stat is in the **Available to testers** status.
+6. After you test the stat, click **Review and publish**. This republishes your game, along with all your updated stats.

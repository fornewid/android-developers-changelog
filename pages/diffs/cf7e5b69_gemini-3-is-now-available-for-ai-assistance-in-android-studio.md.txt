[Android Studio](https://developer.android.com/studio.md.txt) is the best place for professional Android developers to use Gemini 3 for superior performance in [Agent Mode](https://developer.android.com/studio/gemini/agent-mode.md.txt), streamlined development workflows, and advanced problem solving capabilities. With [agentic AI assistance](https://developer.android.com/gemini-in-android) to help you with boilerplate and complex development tasks, Android Studio helps you focus on what you do best---creating high quality apps for your users.
 
 To get started with Gemini 3 Pro for Android development, download or update to the latest version of Android Studio Otter. For developers using Gemini in Android Studio at no-cost (Default Model), we are rolling out limited access to Gemini 3 with a 1 million token size window. For higher usage rate limits and longer sessions with Agent Mode, [use a Gemini API key](https://developer.android.com/studio/gemini/add-api-key.md.txt) to leverage Gemini 3 in Android Studio for the highest tier of AI capability.

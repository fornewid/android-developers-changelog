 ### Choose a surface type
 
-The `surface_type` attribute of `PlayerView` lets you set the type of surface used for video playback. The allowed values are `surface_view`, `texture_view`, `spherical_gl_surface_view` (which is a special value for spherical video playback), `video_decoder_gl_surface_view` (which is for video rendering using extension renderers) and `none` (for audio playback only). More information on which surface type to pick can be found [on the Surface page](https://developer.android.com/media/media3/ui/surface).

+- **Unprocessed reward:** Existing rewards must be [processed](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/integrate.md.txt#process) (acknowledged or consumed) before additional rewards can be granted. Open your game and verify that the previous reward has been processed.

-#### Why did my test purchase fail in the Play Express overlay?

-- Ensure that your test device is running Android 12 (API level 31) or higher and has the latest version of the Google Play Store installed.

+- **Account consistency:** Verify that the account signed in to Play Billing Lab, the Google Play Store app, and your game is the exact same licensed tester account.
+- **Out-of-app purchase handling:** Check your game's `queryPurchasesAsync` implementation and your backend RTDN webhook and API integration. Because rewards are granted outside your app's standard UI, your game or backend server must detect unacknowledged purchases and process them.

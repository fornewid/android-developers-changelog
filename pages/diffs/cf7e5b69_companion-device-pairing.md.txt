+> [!NOTE]
+> **Note:** On Android 14 (API level 34) and higher, you can retrieve the associated device in [`onAssociationCreated()`](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/companion/CompanionDeviceManager.Callback.md.txt#onAssociationCreated(android.companion.AssociationInfo)) using [`AssociationInfo.getAssociatedDevice()`](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/companion/AssociationInfo.md.txt#getAssociatedDevice()), which isn't persisted across sessions. If you instead pass [`AssociationInfo.getDeviceMacAddress()`](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/companion/AssociationInfo.md.txt#getDeviceMacAddress()) to [`BluetoothAdapter.getRemoteDevice()`](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/bluetooth/BluetoothAdapter.md.txt#getRemoteDevice(java.lang.String)) or [`BluetoothAdapter.getRemoteLeDevice()`](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/bluetooth/BluetoothAdapter.md.txt#getRemoteLeDevice(java.lang.String,%20int)), convert [`MacAddress.toString()`](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/net/MacAddress.md.txt#toString()) to uppercase first, such as with `uppercase(Locale.ROOT)`. `MacAddress.toString()` returns a lowercase hexadecimal string, while `BluetoothAdapter` requires uppercase and throws an [`IllegalArgumentException`](https://developer.android.com/reference/java/lang/IllegalArgumentException.md.txt) otherwise.

 ## Compose Material3 Version 1.5
 
+### Version 1.5.0-beta01
+
+October 07, 2026
+
+`androidx.compose.material3:material3-*:1.5.0-beta01` is released. Version 1.5.0-beta01 contains [these commits](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/support/+log/b2b5ae7b0fe1bb4eece7018878fe5f18c5967a2f..19991f2c0858aadfcb442ce02b2c8e4743c96898/compose/material3).
+
+**API Changes**
+
+- Restored `TopAppBarDefaults.enterAlwaysScrollBehavior` overload taking `reverseLayout` as deprecated with warning to maintain compatibility with 1.4. ([I4a87a](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/I4a87a01c8f6a1c616be56d61ff472021c6797f4b))
+- Restored `DraggableState` on `SliderState` and retained `Slider`, `VerticalSlider`, and `RangeSlider` overloads with nullable `onValueChange` as hidden deprecated APIs to maintain binary compatibility. ([Ie4aee](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/Ie4aee051062a922220fbe747b43d02955ced680c))
+- Updated `PolygonShape` APIs: moved `CornerRounding` to top-level with `dp()` and `fraction()` factories, replaced `transformed()` and `scaledToFit()` extensions with in-place mutable `PolygonShape.transform(PolygonShapeTransformScope.() -> Unit)` and `PolygonShape.copy()`. ([If5970](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/If5970afe91c37146e7de4521ca916e59d31b924e))
+- `HorizontalCenteredHeroCarousel`'s `maxItemWidth` parameter has been renamed to `preferredItemWidth`. ([Ie7883](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/Ie7883232c6428c649335c1bcbdd6d51057dc0304))
+- Added `CarouselDefaults.ItemSpacing` and `CarouselDefaults.ContentPadding`, which hold the default item spacing and content padding used by the carousel composables. ([I2f36a](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/I2f36abfea23f415297488cbcbfa3cc69a186ebe8))

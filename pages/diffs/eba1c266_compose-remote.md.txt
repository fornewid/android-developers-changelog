+- Promoted `RemotePaint` interface to public. ([Ida62b](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/Ida62b8e1c51de02aa174f63c41eb31ca75d670f4), [b/465743273](https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/465743273))
+- Removed deprecated shorthand comparison operators (`eq`, `ne`, `lt`, `le`, `gt`, `ge`) across `RemoteFloat`, `RemoteInt`, and `RemoteBoolean`. ([Ie01a9](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/Ie01a909f914ccef2ef1f11e28cd7822e0d8bae79), [b/532022248](https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/532022248), [b/532022248](https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/532022248))

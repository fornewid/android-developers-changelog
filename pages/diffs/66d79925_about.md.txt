+### Reliability fix for dynamic rules
+
+In September 2026, we fixed an issue in Google Play services that
+intermittently prevented some devices running Android 15 (API level 35) and
+higher from applying the dynamic rules in your `assetlinks.json` file. When the
+issue occurred, affected devices used only the intent filters declared in your
+app manifest, so URLs excluded by your dynamic rules could open your app. You
+don't need to change your app or your `assetlinks.json` file. With the fix, we
+expect most devices to apply your dynamic rules as you declared them.

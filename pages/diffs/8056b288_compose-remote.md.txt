+### Version 1.0.0-alpha17
+
+August 12, 2026
+
+`androidx.compose.remote:remote-*:1.0.0-alpha17` is released. Version 1.0.0-alpha17 contains [these commits](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/support/+log/61ee8cd421d0c0252d8db0253b739de537999371..e25ec03c7788f3a71bd0290253e35cb311fa4f5f/compose/remote).

+**API Changes**
+
+- Promoted several `RemoteCompose` unit and type conversion APIs to public, including `toRemoteFloat()`, `toRemoteLong()`, `toPx(density)`, `asRdp()`, `toRemoteDp()`, `asRemoteTextUnit()`, and `toRsp()`. ([Idca36](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/Idca36af6cab36eb22de99b70d45a4cb367659252), [b/465743273](https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/465743273))
+- Promoted `drawCircle`, `drawOval`, and `drawLine` in `RemoteDrawScope` to public. ([Iccf34](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/Iccf346a83f6830b6f37935909e762996fbe8bf46), [b/465743273](https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/465743273))
+- Added arithmetic and comparison operations to `RemoteDp`. ([I4b712](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/I4b7124d5e9a18fb48847028cbd28771c7b715976), [b/467023174](https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/467023174))
+- Exposed `RemoteBrush`, `radialGradient`, and `sweepGradient` as public APIs in remote-creation-compose. ([I24695](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/I24695287ebc231f83aa384c38418eeece4493882), [b/465743273](https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/465743273))
+
+**Other**
+
+- Move to default `minSdk` 24. ([I972da](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/I972dab1c69d8d9ab1fe5a4dd73e07a4ddedd413d), [b/474169350](https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/474169350))
+- Disallow parent touch interception when `RemoteComposeView` handles touch. ([I4c6cd](https://android-review.googlesource.com/#/q/I4c6cd250030696ffa246023fc3a2d86591708b76), [b/514549600](https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/514549600))

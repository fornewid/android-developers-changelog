-### Manage Game Stats visibility on your device for testing
-
-Once the game's available to your testers in the Play Console, follow these
-steps to view the [Game Stats](https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/gamestats) on your device:
-
-1. Clear the Google Play Store cache:
-
-   1. Open the **Settings** app on your device.
-   2. Tap **Apps** \> **Google Play Store**.
-   3. Tap **Storage and cache** , and then tap **Clear cache**.
-2. Open the **Google Play Store** app.
-
-3. View your game stats using either the **Track your playtime** section or
-   your **gamer profile**:
-
-#### Method 1: Use the Track your playtime section
-
-Go to the **Track your playtime** section, and then tap it to view your game
-stats.
-[![Game Stats Test Method 1 to use the track your playtime section.](https://developer.android.com/static/images/games/pgs/gamestats-test-method1.png)](https://developer.android.com/static/images/games/pgs/gamestats-test-method1.png) The Track your playtime section on the You tab.
-
-#### Method 2: Use your gamer profile
-
-1. Tap the **You** tab, and then tap your gamer profile.
-2. Go to the **Game Stats** section.
-3. For the time range, select **All**.
-4. If your game doesn't appear, tap **See all games**.
-5. Select your game to view its stats.
-
-[![Game Stats Test Method 2 to use the gamer profile.](https://developer.android.com/static/images/games/pgs/gamestats-test-method2.png)](https://developer.android.com/static/images/games/pgs/gamestats-test-method2.png) The Game Stats section on the gamer profile.
-
-> [!NOTE]
-> **Note:** Because the UI in the **You** tab's available only for testing purposes, it might take up to 3 days for stats to appear after you add internal test accounts in the Google Play Console. If **See all games** doesn't appear, play at least four games for more than 5 minutes each.

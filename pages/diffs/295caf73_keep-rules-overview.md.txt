+## Understand how keep rules affect optimization
+
+R8 is a whole-program optimizer that analyzes your app code to remove unused
+code, inline methods, merge classes, and obfuscate identifiers. However, R8 can
+only optimize within the configuration you define.
+
+Keep rules represent an explicit instruction to limit optimization. Every keep
+rule you declare restricts R8's ability to transform that portion of your
+codebase. The value of a well-crafted keep rule lies in its specificity:
+
+- **Narrow keep rules enable maximum optimization:** By targeting only the
+  exact entry points accessed dynamically (such as a reflective constructor
+  or a serialized field), you let R8 optimize the rest of your code.
+
+- **Broad keep rules prevent optimization:** Declaring package-wide
+  rules or wide wildcards prevents R8 from optimizing entire classes and all
+  of their members. R8 can't remove, inline, merge, or obfuscate that code.
+
+Overly broad keep rules directly lead to bloated DEX binaries, increased app
+memory footprint, slower startup times (TTID and TTFD), and higher ANR rates.
+Your goal shouldn't be to keep classes "just in case," but to define the minimal
+contract required for dynamic execution.
+
+To find broad or unnecessary keep rules, including those from third-party
+libraries, use the [R8 Configuration Analyzer](https://developer.android.com/topic/performance/app-optimization/r8-configuration-analyzer.md.txt). It reports shrinking,
+optimization, and obfuscation scores that show how much of your code R8 can
+optimize.

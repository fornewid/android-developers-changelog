+

+- Fixed multiple issues including: improved memory management in `XrInstanceManager` by using `applicationContext`, resolved potential lifecycle-related issues by simplifying `Session` and `Scene` caching mechanisms, and enhanced the `ReferenceCleaner` to ensure thread-safe cleanup registrations and explicit disposals.

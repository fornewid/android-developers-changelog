+### Scrubber and seek bar controls
+
+When implementing a custom seek bar or scrubber that connects to an `ExoPlayer`
+instance, enable scrubbing mode with
+[`ExoPlayer.setScrubbingModeEnabled`](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/media3/exoplayer/ExoPlayer.md.txt#setScrubbingModeEnabled(boolean)) when the user starts
+dragging the scrubber thumb, dispatch `Player.seekTo` updates during the drag,
+and disable scrubbing mode as soon as the gesture finishes or is canceled. Avoid
+leaving scrubbing mode enabled during normal playback or single seeks, because
+it suppresses playback and increases decoder resource usage.
+
+We also recommend configuring a `dp`-based fractional seek tolerance from your
+UI scrubber's width using
+`ScrubbingModeParameters.Builder.setFractionalSeekTolerance` so ExoPlayer can
+snap to nearby keyframes within the touch precision of the scrubber. For code
+examples and details, see [Scrubbing mode](https://developer.android.com/media/media3/exoplayer/scrubbing.md.txt).
